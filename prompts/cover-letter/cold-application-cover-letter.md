# Cold Application Cover Letter Generator

> Write a compelling outreach letter to prospective employers or hiring managers when no public job posting is currently advertised.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt crafts an engaging, value-focused inquiry letter (or expanded email) designed to introduce your background to prospective employers, inquire about unadvertised openings, and secure an exploratory conversation.

## Best For

- Speculative applications and networking outreach.
- Inquiring about unlisted or upcoming roles at companies you admire.
- Pitching specialized skills directly to department heads or recruiters.

## Information You Need

- Candidate Name & Target Field
- Recipient Name / Title (e.g., VP of Engineering, Marketing Director)
- Company Name
- Core Value Proposition / Key Accomplishments

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as a strategic career outreach specialist and professional copywriter.

TASK:
Write a cold application letter / speculative email introducing my background to a department head at [COMPANY_NAME] and requesting an exploratory conversation.

INPUT DATA:
- Candidate Name: [YOUR_NAME]
- Target Role / Department: [TARGET_JOB_TITLE]
- Recipient Title / Name: [RECIPIENT_NAME]
- Company Name: [COMPANY_NAME]
- My Core Value Proposition: [YOUR_RELEVANT_SKILLS]
- Key Career Accomplishments: [YOUR_EXPERIENCE]
- Reason for Interest in Company: [WHY_THIS_COMPANY]

LETTER STRUCTURE:
1. Subject Line Ideas: Provide 3 clear, professional subject lines.
2. Opening: Direct, respectful greeting addressing the recipient, noting admiration for the company's work.
3. The Value Pitch: Briefly introduce your core expertise and share 1-2 relevant, high-impact achievements.
4. The Ask: Propose a short 15-minute phone call or coffee chat to discuss potential alignment or upcoming team growth.
5. Sign-Off: Professional closing with contact links.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Do NOT sound pushy, entitled, or desperate.
- Keep the main body under 200 words.
- Do not invent experience or metrics.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
3 Subject Line Options followed by the complete outreach email body in Markdown.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block above.
2. Fill in the placeholders with details about your background and target manager.
3. Generate the letter and customize the subject line.
4. Send via email or LinkedIn message.

## Expected Output

Three strong email subject line ideas alongside a short, impact-focused speculative outreach email.

## Tips

- Keep the outreach short—busy managers appreciate brevity.
- Follow up once after 5 to 7 business days if you receive no response.

## Notes

- Speculative outreach works best when you clearly demonstrate how your skills solve a current operational need.

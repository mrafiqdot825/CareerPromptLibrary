# Self-Performance Review Writing Assistant

> Draft a compelling, evidence-backed self-evaluation performance review that highlights achievements, skills growth, and value delivered.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt helps you write a structured, objective self-performance review. It translates your past year's project notes, accomplishments, and team support into clear review sections: Key Accomplishments, Leadership & Values, Areas of Growth, and Next Year's Goals.

## Best For

- Completing annual or mid-year self-performance evaluations at work.
- Building a documented record of accomplishments to support promotion discussions.
- Framing professional challenges as constructive growth learning experiences.

## Information You Need

- Role Title & Department
- Major Projects Completed & Key Outcomes
- Skill Development Milestones
- Goals for the Next Evaluation Period

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as a senior HR director and executive performance reviewer.

TASK:
Write a comprehensive, professional self-performance review based on my accomplishments, project outcomes, and growth goals.

INPUT DATA:
- Current Role Title: [TARGET_JOB_TITLE]
- Review Period: [REVIEW_PERIOD]
- Accomplishments & Key Projects: [WORK_EXPERIENCE]
- Metrics / Outcomes Achieved: [ACHIEVEMENTS]
- Core Challenges Overcome: [CHALLENGES_OVERCOME]
- Future Development Goals: [FUTURE_GOALS]

REVIEW SECTIONS:
1. Executive Summary: Overview of contributions and core value delivered during the review period.
2. Major Accomplishments & Business Impact (Action Verb + Project Context + Measurable Result).
3. Core Competencies & Company Values Alignment (Examples of teamwork, leadership, and problem-solving).
4. Continuous Improvement & Growth Areas (Constructive framing of skills currently developing).
5. Strategic Goals for Next Review Cycle (3 SMART goals for upcoming period).

CONSTRAINTS:
- Keep tone professional, confident, objective, and evidence-backed.
- Do NOT invent fake achievements or metrics.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Markdown self-performance review document formatted with clear section headers and bullet points.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block above.
2. Fill in your role details, review period, project achievements, challenges, and future goals.
3. Run the prompt in your AI model.
4. Review and copy the formatted review into your company's performance management portal.

## Expected Output

A complete self-evaluation document featuring executive summary, accomplishment bullets, values alignment, and future goals.

## Tips

- Keep a weekly "brag sheet" file throughout the year to record accomplishments as they happen.
- Quantify project impact wherever data is available to make your self-review undeniable.

## Notes

- Detailed self-reviews make it significantly easier for your manager to advocate for your compensation increases.

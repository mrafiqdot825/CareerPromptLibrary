# Freelance Niche & Positioning Finder

> Combine your core skills, industry experience, and client demand to pinpoint profitable, specialized freelance service niches.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt analyzes your skills, interests, and past experience to identify 3 distinct, profitable freelance niche positions. It defines target client avatars, specific high-value service offerings, and positioning statements that reduce competition.

## Best For

- Escaping competitive, low-rate generalist freelancing (e.g., "I am a Web Developer").
- Positioning yourself as a specialized expert (e.g., "I build Shopify stores for Sustainable Skincare Brands").
- Increasing project rates by targeting specific industry client problems.

## Information You Need

- Core Hard Skills & Tools
- Past Industries Worked In / Familiar With
- Personal Interests or Preferred Business Types

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as a freelance business strategist and market positioning consultant.

TASK:
Analyze my skills, experience, and interests to generate 3 high-value, specialized freelance niche positioning options.

INPUT DATA:
- Core Skills & Tools: [SKILLS]
- Past Industry Experience: [WORK_EXPERIENCE]
- Preferred Client / Business Types: [TARGET_CLIENT]

NICHE POSITIONING ANALYSIS:
For each of the 3 niche options, provide:
1. Niche Title & Positioning Statement ("I help [Target Client] solve [Specific Problem] using [Skill/Tool]").
2. Ideal Client Avatar (Who pays for this service and why).
3. Primary High-Value Service Package Offer.
4. Demand & Rate Potential Assessment (Low, Medium, High).
5. 2 Strategic Outreach Channels to find these clients.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Focus on practical, commercially viable niches with proven market demand.
- Avoid overly broad positions (e.g., "General Graphic Designer").

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Markdown analysis report breaking down 3 distinct niche options in comparison tables.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block.
2. Fill in your skills, industry experience, and client preferences.
3. Run the prompt in your AI tool.
4. Choose the niche option that offers the best balance of interest and rate potential.

## Expected Output

Three detailed freelance niche options featuring positioning statements, client avatars, service packages, and outreach channels.

## Tips

- Positioning yourself tightly in a niche allows you to charge premium project rates because clients view you as a domain specialist.
- You can test a niche with 2-3 clients before committing long-term branding to it.

## Notes

- Specialization makes marketing easier and significantly increases project win rates.

# Resume Improvement Assistant

> Critique and polish an existing resume to elevate tone, remove fluff, strengthen weak phrasing, and highlight key accomplishments.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt performs a comprehensive audit of your current resume. It evaluates clarity, tone, conciseness, and action verb strength, providing specific line-by-line improvements alongside an upgraded version of your text.

## Best For

- Elevating an existing draft before applying to competitive positions.
- Eliminating passive voice, corporate buzzwords, and repetitive phrasing.
- Improving overall clarity, readability, and impact.

## Information You Need

- Your Current Resume Text
- Target Job Level or Role

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as an executive recruiter and expert resume reviewer.

TASK:
Critique and improve my current resume text to elevate readability, strengthen active tone, remove filler words, and highlight career impact.

INPUT DATA:
- Target Role / Level: [TARGET_JOB_TITLE]
- Current Resume Content: [WORK_EXPERIENCE]

IMPROVEMENT DIRECTIVES:
1. Identify and replace passive voice (e.g., "Responsible for managing") with strong action verbs (e.g., "Spearheaded", "Engineered", "Directed").
2. Remove vague corporate buzzwords, clichés, and filler phrases.
3. Improve sentence structure for impact while retaining 100% factual accuracy.
4. Ensure bullet points are punchy, concise, and focused on value delivery.

OUTPUT STRUCTURE:
1. Executive Critique (Strengths, Weaknesses, Buzzwords to Remove)
2. Improved Resume Draft (Clean Markdown)
3. Line-by-Line Highlight of Major Changes Made
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block above.
2. Paste your current resume text into `[WORK_EXPERIENCE]`.
3. Run the prompt in your AI assistant.
4. Review the critique and adopt the improved phrasing.

## Expected Output

An executive critique breaking down areas of improvement, followed by a polished draft and an explanation of key edits.

## Tips

- Compare the "Before" and "After" versions to ensure the new wording retains your authentic voice.
- Use the critique feedback to guide future application updates.

## Notes

- The AI will refine phrasing but will not add metrics unless you provide them.

# Complete LinkedIn Profile Optimizer

> Transform your full LinkedIn profile into a magnetic personal brand landing page optimized for recruiter search algorithms (SEO).

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt reviews and rewrites all major components of your LinkedIn profile—Headline, About section, Featured recommendations, Experience descriptions, and Skills keywords—to maximize recruiter search visibility and professional credibility.

## Best For

- Conducting a full LinkedIn profile refresh before launching a job search.
- Optimizing profile keywords so recruiters discover you in LinkedIn Recruiter searches.
- Building a cohesive personal brand statement across your profile.

## Information You Need

- Current Resume or Work History
- Target Job Title & Industry
- Top 5 Core Technical & Functional Skills
- Target Audience (Recruiters, Hiring Managers, Potential Clients)

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as a LinkedIn personal branding specialist and expert talent acquisition consultant.

TASK:
Overhaul my complete LinkedIn profile to maximize SEO searchability, recruiter engagement, and professional positioning.

INPUT DATA:
- Target Job Title: [TARGET_JOB_TITLE]
- Target Industry: [TARGET_INDUSTRY]
- Current Work History & Experience: [WORK_EXPERIENCE]
- Core Skills & Competencies: [SKILLS]
- Key Career Accomplishments: [ACHIEVEMENTS]

PROFILE SECTIONS TO GENERATE:
1. HEADLINE: 3 searchable options (under 220 characters) combining job title, domain expertise, and core value proposition.
2. ABOUT SECTION: First-person narrative (250–350 words) structured with an engaging hook, core story, key specialties bullet list, and call-to-action.
3. EXPERIENCE SECTION: Reformatted bullet points tailored for online reading (first-person active voice, strong formatting).
4. TOP 5 SKILLS TO FEATURE: Primary keywords for endorsement alignment.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Write in an authentic, natural human voice.
- Do NOT fabricate metrics, titles, or experiences.
- Avoid robotic buzzwords ("guru", "ninja", "passionate synergistic leader").

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Fully formatted Markdown text organized clearly by profile section.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block above.
2. Fill in your role details, experience, skills, and target industry.
3. Run the prompt in your AI model.
4. Copy each optimized section into your LinkedIn edit fields.

## Expected Output

An end-to-end LinkedIn profile transformation containing 3 headline options, a full About summary, reformatted Experience entries, and top skills.

## Tips

- Set your profile privacy to "Open to Work" (visible to recruiters only, if currently employed).
- Ensure your profile picture is high-resolution, professional, and well-lit.

## Notes

- LinkedIn Recruiter relies heavily on exact keyword matches in Headlines, About sections, and Skill tags.

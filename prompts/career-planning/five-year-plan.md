# Five-Year Career & Professional Growth Plan

> Map out a comprehensive 5-year strategic vision balancing title progression, compensation targets, skill mastery, and work-life harmony.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt constructs a year-by-year 5-year strategic development plan. It establishes clear yearly objectives across role titles, compensation growth, skill development, leadership responsibilities, and major portfolio achievements.

## Best For

- Gaining clarity on your 5-year career trajectory.
- Aligning personal life planning with long-term career benchmarks.
- Preparing long-range career narratives for executive or graduate applications.

## Information You Need

- Current Role, Compensation, & Skillset
- 5-Year Vision / Target Role & Industry
- Personal Values & Flexibility Preferences

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as a strategic career advisor and executive mentor.

TASK:
Develop a comprehensive 5-year strategic professional growth plan detailing yearly milestones, skill targets, and career progression steps.

INPUT DATA:
- Current Role: [WORK_EXPERIENCE]
- Current Industry: [TARGET_INDUSTRY]
- 5-Year Vision / Ultimate Role Goal: [CAREER_GOALS]
- Key Professional Priorities: [PRIORITIES]

YEAR-BY-YEAR PLAN FRAMEWORK:
- YEAR 1 (Foundation & Optimization): Mastering current role, filling key skill gaps, establishing network baseline.
- YEAR 2 (Growth & Expanded Impact): Leading high-visibility projects, seeking first promotion or strategic role move.
- YEAR 3 (Mid-Point Transition): Step up into senior individual contributor or management position, expanding scope.
- YEAR 4 (Leadership & Domain Authority): Building team capabilities, driving strategic domain outcomes, public/industry visibility.
- YEAR 5 (Vision Achievement): Transition into target long-term role, evaluating executive/consulting pathways.

FOR EACH YEAR PROVIDE:
1. Primary Goal & Focus Theme
2. Key Skills to Acquire
3. Target Deliverables / Portfolio Accomplishments
4. Key Performance Metric

CONSTRAINTS:
- Maintain realistic, grounded progression assumptions.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Markdown document formatted with year-by-year headers and summary matrix tables.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block.
2. Fill in your current baseline and 5-year vision placeholders.
3. Run the prompt in your AI model.
4. Review the 5-year roadmap annually to track progress.

## Expected Output

A year-by-year 5-year strategic development plan featuring focus themes, skill targets, and performance metrics.

## Tips

- Treat your 5-year plan as a living document; revisit and adjust it as your personal interests evolve.
- Focus heavily on executing Year 1 milestones with high discipline before worrying about Year 4 specifics.

## Notes

- Long-term planning provides direction when short-term career obstacles or market downturns occur.

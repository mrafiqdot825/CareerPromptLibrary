# Cold Client Outreach Pitch Generator

> Craft short, personalized cold outreach emails to introduce your freelance services to potential business clients.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt drafts a 4-paragraph cold outreach email targeting decision-makers (e.g., Marketing Directors, Founders, CTOs). It highlights a specific improvement opportunity for their business, pitches a quick solution, and offers a low-friction call to action.

## Best For

- Pitching freelance services directly to prospective clients outside marketplace platforms.
- Building a private client pipeline through direct outreach.
- Offering audits, teardowns, or specific micro-services.

## Information You Need

- Prospect Name & Company Name
- Specific Opportunity / Audit Observation (e.g., slow mobile site, outdated UI, missing content)
- Service Offered & Value Outcome
- Low-Friction Call to Action (e.g., short audit video, brief 10-minute call)

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as a cold outreach expert and freelance sales strategist.

TASK:
Write a cold outreach email pitch targeting a business owner or department manager at [COMPANY_NAME].

INPUT DATA:
- Prospect Name & Title: [RECIPIENT_NAME]
- Target Company: [COMPANY_NAME]
- Specific Observation / Value Opportunity: [AUDIT_OBSERVATION]
- Service Offered & Value: [SERVICES_OFFERED]
- Low-Friction Offer: [LOW_FRICTION_OFFER]

EMAIL STRUCTURE:
1. Subject Line Options: Provide 3 short, curiosity-inducing subject lines.
2. Personalized Opening: Acknowledge a specific accomplishment or observation about [COMPANY_NAME].
3. The Value Observation: Highlight the specific gap or optimization opportunity identified in your research.
4. The Low-Friction Offer: Offer a free 2-minute video audit or brief tip without hard-selling.
5. Soft Sign-off: Polite sign-off with links to portfolio.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Total email body length MUST be under 150 words.
- Do NOT sound like spam or pushy sales pitch.
- Do not fabricate false audit findings.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
3 Subject line options followed by the cold email pitch in Markdown.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block.
2. Fill in the prospect info, specific audit observation, service, and low-friction offer.
3. Run the prompt in your AI model.
4. Send the pitch via email or LinkedIn message.

## Expected Output

Three subject line options accompanied by a short cold pitch email focused on value.

## Tips

- Personalize the opening observation for every recipient—generic cold templates get marked as spam.
- Offer a low-friction asset (e.g., "Would it be alright if I sent over a 2-minute video explaining how to fix this?") before asking for a sales meeting.

## Notes

- Low-friction offers build trust and significantly increase cold outreach response rates.

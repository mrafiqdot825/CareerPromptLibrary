# Questions to Ask the Interviewer Generator

> Generate strategic, insightful questions to ask interviewers that demonstrate business acumen, role preparation, and genuine interest.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt creates a tailored list of questions for you to ask at the end of an interview. Categorized by interviewer role (Recruiter, Hiring Manager, Peer Team Member, Executive Leader), these questions help evaluate team culture and leaves a lasting impression.

## Best For

- Preparing for the "Do you have any questions for us?" portion of an interview.
- Uncovering team health, expectations, challenges, and growth opportunities.
- Demonstrating strategic thinking and domain engagement.

## Information You Need

- Target Job Title & Company Name
- Interviewer Title / Role (Recruiter, Hiring Manager, Department Head)
- Job Description Summary

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as a senior executive career coach and hiring strategist.

TASK:
Generate a strategic list of insightful questions for me to ask my interviewer based on the target role and company context.

INPUT DATA:
- Target Job Title: [TARGET_JOB_TITLE]
- Company Name: [COMPANY_NAME]
- Interview Round / Interviewer Role: [RECIPIENT_NAME]
- Job Posting Overview: [JOB_DESCRIPTION]

QUESTION CATEGORIES:
1. Role Success & Expectations (Clarifying 30/60/90-day targets and key performance metrics).
2. Team Culture & Operational Reality (Uncovering communication workflows, challenges, and team dynamics).
3. Strategic Vision & Growth (Exploring business priorities, expansion plans, and technical roadmaps).
4. Interviewer Personal Experience (Questions that engage the interviewer about their experience at the company).

CONSTRAINTS:
- Avoid basic questions that could be answered by reading the company's homepage.
- Keep questions open-ended and professional.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Markdown report categorized by category with brief context notes explaining *why* each question is impactful.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block.
2. Fill in `[TARGET_JOB_TITLE]`, `[COMPANY_NAME]`, `[RECIPIENT_NAME]`, and `[JOB_DESCRIPTION]`.
3. Run the prompt in your AI model.
4. Select 3-4 questions to bring to your interview session.

## Expected Output

A categorized list of strategic questions accompanied by context notes explaining what each question reveals about the employer.

## Tips

- Choose questions that genuinely interest you—interviewers sense authentic curiosity.
- Take notes during the interviewer's response to demonstrate active listening.

## Notes

- Thoughtful questions distinguish top candidates from average applicants during final evaluation decisions.

# Promotion & Salary Raise Business Case Generator

> Construct a data-backed business case document and conversation script for requesting a title promotion or compensation raise.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt builds a formal proposal document and meeting script requesting a role promotion or salary adjustment. It documents expanded responsibilities, business value delivered, market benchmark data, and formal title change requests.

## Best For

- Preparing for formal promotion discussions during 1-on-1s or performance reviews.
- Making a compelling, objective business case for compensation adjustment.
- Approaching compensation conversations with confidence and structured data.

## Information You Need

- Current Role Title vs Target Role Title
- Expanded Scope / Responsibilities Taken On
- Business Achievements & Impact Metrics
- Market Salary Benchmarks (if available)

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as an executive compensation advisor and career strategist.

TASK:
Build a data-backed promotion business case document and 1-on-1 meeting conversation script requesting a title advancement and compensation adjustment.

INPUT DATA:
- Current Role Title: [WORK_EXPERIENCE]
- Target Role Title: [TARGET_JOB_TITLE]
- Company Name: [COMPANY_NAME]
- Expanded Responsibilities Taken On: [EXPANDED_RESPONSIBILITIES]
- Key Business Accomplishments & Impact: [ACHIEVEMENTS]
- Market Salary Benchmark Data: [SALARY_BENCHMARK]

BUSINESS CASE DOCUMENT SECTIONS:
1. Executive Summary: Formal request for title progression to [TARGET_JOB_TITLE].
2. Track Record of Value Delivery: Summary of key projects, revenue impact, or process improvements.
3. Expanded Scope Matrix: Comparison table showing current duties vs target role duties already being performed.
4. Market Benchmark Alignment: Contextualizing compensation alignment with industry rates.

CONVERSATION SCRIPT:
- 1-on-1 Verbal Opening Script: Natural, confident 60-second intro to initiate the conversation with your manager.
- Handling Potential Objections: 2 common manager responses (e.g., budget constraints, timing) with professional counter-responses.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Do NOT sound demanding, entitled, or ultimatum-driven.
- Focus heavily on business value already delivered and expanded responsibility scope.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Markdown business case document followed by the verbal conversation script and objection handling guide.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block.
2. Fill in your title transition, expanded scope, accomplishments, and market benchmark data.
3. Run the prompt in your AI model.
4. Share the business case document with your manager before your scheduled meeting and practice the verbal opening script out loud.

## Expected Output

A 2-part packet containing a formal promotion business case document and a verbal meeting script with objection handling advice.

## Tips

- Frame your request around the fact that you are *already operating* at the higher role level.
- Schedule promotion conversations 2-3 months before fiscal budget cycles close so funds can be allocated.

## Notes

- Data-backed business cases make it easy for your manager to get approval from HR and higher executives.

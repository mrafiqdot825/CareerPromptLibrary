# Post-Interview Debrief & Feedback Analyzer

> Conduct a structured self-debrief immediately after an interview to evaluate answer quality, note follow-up items, and draft tailored thank-you emails.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt helps you process a completed interview while memories are fresh. It analyzes questions asked, highlights answer strengths and weaknesses, identifies topics needing clarification, and generates a personalized thank-you follow-up email.

## Best For

- Conducting an immediate post-interview debrief within 2 hours of completing a round.
- Preparing tailored thank-you notes that reference specific conversation details.
- Improving performance for subsequent interview rounds.

## Information You Need

- Company Name & Interviewer Name/Title
- Questions Asked During Interview
- Summary of Your Given Answers
- Key Conversation Highlights or Mutual Interests Mentioned

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as a career counselor and executive debrief coach.

TASK:
Analyze my completed interview round, identify areas for improvement in subsequent rounds, and draft a personalized thank-you email to the interviewer.

INPUT DATA:
- Target Job Title: [TARGET_JOB_TITLE]
- Company Name: [COMPANY_NAME]
- Interviewer Name & Title: [RECIPIENT_NAME]
- Key Questions Asked & My Answers: [WORK_EXPERIENCE]
- Specific Discussion Highlights / Notes: [JOB_DESCRIPTION]

DEBRIEF ANALYSIS:
1. Performance Strengths: What went well and resonated with the interviewer.
2. Areas of Concern / Missteps: Questions where answers were weak, rambling, or incomplete.
3. Preparation Focus for Next Round: Specific topics to review or practice.
4. Personalized Thank-You Email: A concise, professional follow-up email (under 150 words) referencing a specific insight discussed during the call.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Keep the thank-you email sincere, professional, and free of desperation.
- Ensure email is sent within 24 hours of interview completion.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Debrief evaluation report followed by the ready-to-send thank-you email draft in Markdown.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block.
2. Fill in the interview details, questions remembered, and conversation highlights.
3. Run the prompt in your AI model.
4. Use the feedback to prep for the next round and send the thank-you email within 24 hours.

## Expected Output

An objective performance debrief coupled with a custom thank-you email referencing specific interview discussion points.

## Tips

- Jot down key questions and topics immediately after logging off your interview call before details fade.
- Use thank-you emails to briefly clarify any answer you felt was incomplete during the call.

## Notes

- Timely thank-you notes reinforce professional courtesy and keep your candidacies top-of-mind.

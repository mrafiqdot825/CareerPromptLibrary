# Workplace Professional Email Assistant

> Draft clear, concise, professional workplace emails for sensitive, assertive, or complex professional scenarios.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt generates professional email responses across common workplace situations: setting boundaries, following up on delayed deliverables, delivering constructive feedback, or requesting project resources.

## Best For

- Navigating tricky or sensitive workplace email communications.
- Maintaining a professional, calm, firm, and respectful tone under pressure.
- Saving time drafting routine or complex business correspondence.

## Information You Need

- Scenario / Context (e.g., Scope creep, missed deadline, project status inquiry)
- Recipient Role (e.g., Manager, Client, Peer Team Member)
- Desired Outcome / Call to Action

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as an executive communications specialist and corporate manager.

TASK:
Write a clear, professional, well-structured workplace email tailored to the provided scenario and desired outcome.

INPUT DATA:
- Communication Scenario: [WORKPLACE_SCENARIO]
- Recipient Role / Relationship: [RECIPIENT_ROLE]
- Core Message & Facts to Convey: [FACTS_TO_CONVEY]
- Desired Outcome / Action Requested: [DESIRED_OUTCOME]
- Preferred Tone: [PREFERRED_TONE]

EMAIL COMPOSITION REQUIREMENTS:
1. Subject Line: Provide 2 clear, concise subject line options.
2. Opening: Professional greeting appropriate for the recipient.
3. Message Body: State context clearly, present key facts without emotional charge, and frame next steps.
4. Call to Action: Explicit request or next step with clear timeline.
5. Closing: Professional sign-off.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Avoid passive-aggressive language, emotional venting, or overly defensive phrasing.
- Keep body text concise (under 175 words).

OUTPUT FORMAT:
2 Subject lines followed by the formatted email text in Markdown.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block above.
2. Fill in the scenario, recipient role, facts, desired outcome, and preferred tone.
3. Generate the email draft in your AI tool.
4. Review tone and send.

## Expected Output

Two subject line options accompanied by a professional, concise email draft.

## Tips

- Re-read emails before sending to verify that emotional tone is neutral and professional.
- Focus on facts, objective impact, and proposed solutions rather than placing blame.

## Notes

- Clear, concise workplace emails resolve issues faster and build a reputation for professional maturity.

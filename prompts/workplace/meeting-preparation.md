# Meeting Agenda & Preparation Brief Generator

> Prepare structured meeting agendas, executive briefing notes, and discussion frameworks to run efficient, high-value meetings.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt transforms raw meeting goals into a structured meeting packet: agenda topics with time allocations, pre-meeting preparation tasks, discussion framing, and action item tracking templates.

## Best For

- Leading team meetings, project kickoffs, or client check-ins.
- Ensuring meetings start on time, stay on topic, and achieve clear decisions.
- Preparing executive briefing notes when presenting to leadership.

## Information You Need

- Meeting Goal / Objective
- Key Attendees & Roles
- Allocated Meeting Duration (e.g., 30 mins, 60 mins)
- Background Context / Key Issues to Resolve

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as a project manager and executive meeting facilitator.

TASK:
Develop a structured meeting agenda and preparation brief based on the target meeting goal and available duration.

INPUT DATA:
- Meeting Objective: [MEETING_OBJECTIVE]
- Meeting Duration: [MEETING_DURATION]
- Attendees & Roles: [ATTENDEES]
- Context & Key Issues: [BACKGROUND_CONTEXT]

MEETING PACKET STRUCTURE:
1. Executive Objective Statement (1 sentence on why this meeting is taking place).
2. Pre-Meeting Prep Tasks (What attendees must review prior to joining).
3. Timed Agenda Table (Topic, Allocated Minutes, Discussion Lead, Desired Deliverable).
4. Key Discussion Questions to Guide Decisions.
5. Action Item & Decision Log Template for post-meeting follow-up.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Allocate 5 minutes at the end specifically for action item assignment and recap.
- Keep agenda realistic for the allotted time.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Markdown meeting briefing document ready to distribute to meeting participants.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block.
2. Fill in meeting objective, duration, attendees, and background context.
3. Run the prompt in your AI assistant.
4. Send the agenda to participants 24 hours prior to the call.

## Expected Output

A timed meeting agenda, pre-meeting task list, discussion prompts, and action item template formatted in Markdown.

## Tips

- Send agendas at least 24 hours in advance so attendees arrive prepared.
- Enforce timed agenda blocks gently during the meeting to keep discussions moving forward.

## Notes

- Structured agendas prevent meeting bloat and save valuable team hours.

# Scholarship & Grant Personal Statement Generator

> Draft compelling personal statements and essays for academic scholarships, grants, or fellowship applications.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt crafts an authentic 500-word personal statement for scholarship or grant applications. It highlights academic background, financial need or merit, community leadership, and future career impact.

## Best For

- Students applying for undergraduate, graduate, or research scholarships.
- Applicants seeking educational grants, fellowships, or study abroad funding.
- Organizing personal stories into persuasive academic essays.

## Information You Need

- Scholarship Name & Criteria/Prompt
- Academic Background & GPA/Honors
- Key Personal Experiences / Obstacles Overcome
- Career Intentions & Community Impact

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as an academic admissions essay consultant and grant writing advisor.

TASK:
Write a compelling, authentic personal statement essay for a scholarship application based on my background, academic goals, and financial/merit needs.

INPUT DATA:
- Scholarship Name / Criteria: [SCHOLARSHIP_NAME]
- Target Degree / Major: [DEGREE_NAME]
- Academic Background & Achievements: [EDUCATION]
- Personal Background / Challenges Overcome: [PERSONAL_BACKGROUND]
- Future Career Goals & Community Vision: [CAREER_GOALS]

ESSAY STRUCTURE:
1. Introduction: Hooking opening paragraph introducing your academic passion and core personal story.
2. Academic & Leadership Merit: Demonstrating commitment through past projects, grades, or community engagement.
3. Purpose & Financial/Merit Need: Explaining how receiving this scholarship will unlock your potential and clear educational barriers.
4. Future Impact & Conclusion: Connecting your education directly to your long-term goal of giving back to society or your field.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Do NOT fabricate hardships, achievements, or academic records.
- Maintain an inspiring, humble, confident, and professional tone.
- Length: 400 to 600 words.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Full scholarship essay text in clean Markdown format.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block above.
2. Fill in the scholarship details, academic achievements, personal history, and goals.
3. Run the prompt in your AI model.
4. Review the essay, verify details, and adjust for your personal voice.

## Expected Output

A complete 500-word personal statement tailored to the scholarship guidelines.

## Tips

- Share specific, memorable details about challenges overcome rather than making broad general statements.
- Proofread carefully to ensure essay word counts adhere strictly to application limits.

## Notes

- Scholarship committees look for clear connections between financial award support and future community/industry impact.

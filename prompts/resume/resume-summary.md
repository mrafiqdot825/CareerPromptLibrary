# Resume Summary Generator

> Craft tailored, high-impact professional summary statements that immediately grab a hiring manager's attention.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt synthesizes your overall experience, core strengths, and target position into 3 distinct professional summary options (e.g., Executive/Senior Tone, Skills-Focused, and Concise/Direct).

## Best For

- Replacing generic objective statements with modern professional summaries.
- Tailoring your top-of-resume introduction to a specific target role.
- Giving hiring managers an immediate 5-second snapshot of your value.

## Information You Need

- Target Job Title
- Years of Experience
- Core Competencies / Top 3 Skills
- Notable Accomplishments or Industry Background

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as a professional resume editor and talent acquisition manager.

TASK:
Write 3 high-impact professional summary options tailored to my background and target role.

INPUT DATA:
- Target Job Title: [TARGET_JOB_TITLE]
- Years of Experience: [YEARS_OF_EXPERIENCE]
- Primary Field / Industry: [TARGET_INDUSTRY]
- Core Strengths & Key Skills: [SKILLS]
- Major Career Highlights: [ACHIEVEMENTS]

REQUIREMENTS:
- Keep each summary between 3 to 4 concise sentences (50–80 words).
- Avoid generic phrases like "hardworking professional", "synergistic leader", or "out-of-the-box thinker".
- Highlight years of experience, core domain expertise, and the specific value brought to an employer.
- Strictly adhere to factual details provided; do not invent credentials.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Option 1: Executive / Impact-Focused Summary
Option 2: Core Skills & Technical Focus Summary
Option 3: Concise & Modern Direct Summary
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block.
2. Fill in `[TARGET_JOB_TITLE]`, `[YEARS_OF_EXPERIENCE]`, `[SKILLS]`, and `[ACHIEVEMENTS]`.
3. Run the prompt and select the summary option that best fits your target application.

## Expected Output

Three distinct, polished summary options structured by tone and focus area.

## Tips

- Place your chosen summary directly under your name and contact header on your resume.
- Customize keywords in the summary to match each job application.

## Notes

- Keep your summary focused on what you bring to the employer, rather than what you hope to gain from them.

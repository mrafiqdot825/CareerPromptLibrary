# LinkedIn Headline Generator

> Generate targeted, keyword-rich LinkedIn headlines that stand out in search results and clearly communicate your value proposition.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt crafts 5 distinct headline formulas tailored to your target position. It balances official role titles, domain keywords, industry certifications, and concise value statements while staying within LinkedIn's 220-character limit.

## Best For

- Replacing generic default headlines (e.g., "Software Engineer at ACME").
- Increasing profile views from recruiters searching for specific titles or tools.
- Positioning yourself effectively during a career shift or job search.

## Information You Need

- Target Job Title
- Core Technical Skills or Domain Expertise
- Industry / Niche
- Key Certification or Value Highlight (e.g., PMP, AWS Certified, Growth Strategist)

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as a LinkedIn personal brand strategist and recruiter.

TASK:
Write 5 distinct, highly searchable LinkedIn headline options tailored to my target role and industry.

INPUT DATA:
- Target Job Title: [TARGET_JOB_TITLE]
- Core Skills & Technologies: [SKILLS]
- Industry / Sector: [TARGET_INDUSTRY]
- Notable Certifications or Achievements: [CERTIFICATIONS]

HEADLINE FORMULA VARIATIONS:
1. Standard Keyword Focus: [Target Title] | [Core Skill 1] | [Core Skill 2] | [Industry Keyword]
2. Value Proposition Focus: Helping [Target Audience/Business] accomplish [Outcome] through [Domain Skill]
3. Authority & Credentials Focus: [Target Title] + [Certifications/Metrics] | Ex-[Notable Brand/Sector]
4. Modern Clean Minimalist: [Target Role] specializing in [Niche Area]
5. Career Pivot / Transition Focus: Framing background toward [Target Role]

CONSTRAINTS:
- Keep every headline strictly under 220 characters.
- Avoid cringey hype words ("Guru", "Wizard", "Visionary", "Thought Leader").
- Ensure target role titles match common recruiter search queries.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Numbered list of 5 headlines with character counts noted for each.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block above.
2. Replace placeholders with your details.
3. Generate the headline options in your AI tool.
4. Select and paste your favorite option into your LinkedIn profile editor.

## Expected Output

Five categorized headline options under 220 characters, with explicit character counts for each.

## Tips

- Include common title terms recruiters search for rather than internal corporate titles (e.g., "Senior Software Engineer" vs "MTS III").
- Test different headlines every few weeks to monitor profile view analytics.

## Notes

- The headline is the single most indexed text field in LinkedIn search algorithms.

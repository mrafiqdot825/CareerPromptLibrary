# LinkedIn Experience Section Optimizer

> Format work experience entries specifically for online reading on LinkedIn, balancing narrative context with achievement bullet points.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt adapts standard resume work experience entries into LinkedIn-optimized descriptions. It uses short paragraphs, bullet points, and core skill tags designed for quick mobile scanning by recruiters and professional connections.

## Best For

- Updating your LinkedIn Experience entries without dumping dense, unformatted resume blocks.
- Making past achievements easily readable on mobile devices.
- Highlighting core skills and technologies used in each specific role.

## Information You Need

- Company Name, Role Title, & Dates
- Core Job Duties & Responsibilities
- Key Accomplishments & Metrics

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as a LinkedIn profile strategist and talent recruiter.

TASK:
Convert my traditional resume work experience entry into a clean, modern, online-optimized LinkedIn Experience description.

INPUT DATA:
- Company Name: [COMPANY_NAME]
- Role Title: [TARGET_JOB_TITLE]
- Employed Dates: [EMPLOYMENT_DATES]
- Duty Notes & Achievements: [WORK_EXPERIENCE]
- Tools & Skills Used: [SKILLS]

LINKEDIN EXPERIENCE FORMAT:
1. Role Summary (1-2 sentences): Overview of team scope, core mission, and main responsibilities.
2. Key Impact & Accomplishments (3-5 concise bullet points): Action verb + context + outcome.
3. Core Technologies & Skills Tag List: Clean list of tools/competencies utilized in this position.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Use clean ASCII bullet points (• or -) that render cleanly on mobile apps.
- Do NOT fabricate duties or metrics.
- Keep total word count per role entry between 150 and 250 words.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Markdown formatted text ready to copy directly into LinkedIn's Experience description box.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block above.
2. Fill in your role title, company name, experience notes, and skills used.
3. Run the prompt in your AI model.
4. Copy the output into your LinkedIn profile under each respective role.

## Expected Output

A mobile-optimized LinkedIn work experience entry complete with a 2-line role overview, impact bullets, and skill tags.

## Tips

- Tag relevant skills at the bottom of each role entry using LinkedIn's native "Skills" feature.
- Use spacing generously between paragraphs to prevent walls of text.

## Notes

- LinkedIn readers scan profiles faster than static printed resumes; visual structure is critical.

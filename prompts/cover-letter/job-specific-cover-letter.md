# Job-Specific Cover Letter Creator

> Draft a bespoke cover letter that weaves specific company projects, mission values, and key job posting requirements into your career story.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt creates a deeply customized cover letter. It analyzes why you want to work at the target company specifically, connects your past successes to their current initiatives, and addresses their key operational needs directly.

## Best For

- Applications to priority target companies or dream roles.
- Showing deep research into company culture, recent news, or core values.
- Differentiating yourself from candidates sending generic template letters.

## Information You Need

- Candidate Name & Experience Highlights
- Target Job Title & Description
- Company Name & Specific Reason for Targeting Company (e.g., product line, mission, culture)

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as a senior executive coach and persuasive career copywriter.

TASK:
Write a highly targeted, bespoke cover letter connecting my specific background to [COMPANY_NAME]'s mission, culture, and key job requirements.

INPUT DATA:
- Candidate Name: [YOUR_NAME]
- Target Job Title: [TARGET_JOB_TITLE]
- Company Name: [COMPANY_NAME]
- Why This Company: [WHY_THIS_COMPANY]
- Relevant Experience & Strengths: [YOUR_EXPERIENCE]
- Job Posting Highlights: [JOB_DESCRIPTION]

WRITING REQUIREMENTS:
- Open with a compelling statement that links your career passion or domain expertise directly to [COMPANY_NAME]'s vision.
- Highlight 2 specific pain points mentioned in the job description and demonstrate how your experience solves them.
- Weave in the "Why This Company" context naturally so the letter cannot be repurposed for any other employer.
- Maintain a warm, confident, professional human tone.

CONSTRAINTS:
- No corporate buzzwords or generic fluff.
- No fabricated facts or claims.
- Length: 250–350 words maximum.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Full tailored cover letter formatted cleanly in Markdown.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block above.
2. Fill in `[COMPANY_NAME]`, `[WHY_THIS_COMPANY]`, `[YOUR_EXPERIENCE]`, and `[JOB_DESCRIPTION]`.
3. Generate the response in your AI tool.
4. Review for authentic voice and tone alignment.

## Expected Output

A custom-crafted cover letter that uniquely reflects your interest in the specific employer.

## Tips

- Check the company's website about page or recent news releases to find genuine points of interest for `[WHY_THIS_COMPANY]`.
- Focus on how you can contribute value rather than just what the company can do for your career.

## Notes

- Highly tailored cover letters significantly increase recruiter engagement for competitive positions.

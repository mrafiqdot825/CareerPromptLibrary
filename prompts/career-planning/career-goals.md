# SMART Career Goal Planner

> Convert general career ambitions into structured SMART goals with clear milestones, measurement metrics, and execution steps.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt takes your career ambitions (e.g., "I want to become a manager" or "I want to earn more money") and translates them into actionable SMART goals: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound.

## Best For

- Preparing for annual performance reviews or goal-setting sessions with your manager.
- Establishing clear quarterly benchmarks for personal and professional growth.
- Turning overwhelming long-term aspirations into manageable daily routines.

## Information You Need

- Current Role & Core Ambition
- Target Timeline (e.g., 6 months, 1 year)
- Resource Constraints / Available Time

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as an executive performance coach and goal-setting strategist.

TASK:
Convert my high-level career ambition into a structured SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) goal action plan.

INPUT DATA:
- Current Role: [WORK_EXPERIENCE]
- Career Ambition / Objective: [CAREER_GOALS]
- Desired Completion Timeframe: [TARGET_TIMELINE]
- Available Time / Resources: [WEEKLY_HOURS_AVAILABLE]

SMART GOAL BREAKDOWN:
1. Specific: Define precisely what success looks like.
2. Measurable: Identify explicit metrics or deliverables that prove completion.
3. Achievable: Verify feasibility given current resources and setup.
4. Relevant: Connect this goal directly to broader career trajectory.
5. Time-Bound: Establish key quarterly and monthly milestones.
6. Execution Routine: 3 habit-based actions to take weekly.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Keep goals realistic, actionable, and free of vague motivational jargon.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Markdown goal sheet structured with clear section headers, milestone tables, and weekly execution habits.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block above.
2. Fill in your current role, broad ambition, timeframe, and weekly availability.
3. Generate the SMART goal sheet in your AI assistant.
4. Print or save the milestone table to track your progress monthly.

## Expected Output

A comprehensive SMART goal document complete with clear metrics, quarterly milestones, and weekly habits.

## Tips

- Review your SMART goals monthly to celebrate progress and adjust timelines if obstacles arise.
- Align your SMART goals with your organization's quarterly objectives for maximum promotion visibility.

## Notes

- Specific, time-bound goals increase execution success rates compared to unmeasured resolutions.

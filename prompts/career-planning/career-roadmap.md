# Strategic Career Roadmap Planner

> Construct a clear, multi-stage career progression roadmap detailing skill milestones, role stepping stones, and estimated timelines.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt maps out a step-by-step career path from your current position to a desired future role. It breaks down intermediate job titles, key technical skills to acquire, certifications needed, and realistic milestone timeframes.

## Best For

- Planning long-term professional progression over 2 to 5 years.
- Understanding intermediate stepping-stone roles required to reach senior or executive titles.
- Establishing clear skill development priorities.

## Information You Need

- Current Job Title & Experience Level
- Desired Target Role (e.g., Engineering Manager, VP of Product, Senior Cloud Architect)
- Core Skills Currently Possessed
- Industry / Field Focus

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as a senior career development strategist and executive talent consultant.

TASK:
Develop a realistic, multi-stage career roadmap detailing the path from my current position to my long-term target role.

INPUT DATA:
- Current Job Title: [WORK_EXPERIENCE]
- Target Long-Term Role: [TARGET_JOB_TITLE]
- Industry / Sector: [TARGET_INDUSTRY]
- Years of Experience: [YEARS_OF_EXPERIENCE]
- Current Core Skills: [SKILLS]

ROADMAP COMPONENTS:
1. Stepping-Stone Progression Stages: 2-3 intermediate roles/milestones between current position and target role.
2. Skill & Competency Roadmap per Stage (Hard Skills, Soft Skills, Leadership capabilities).
3. Recommended Credentials & Certifications.
4. Strategic Projects & Leadership Opportunities to seek in current role to trigger promotion.
5. Realistic Timeline Estimates per stage.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Do not make unrealistic promises (e.g., "From Junior Developer to CTO in 12 months").
- Keep trajectory stages grounded in industry standards.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Markdown roadmap document featuring a visual ASCII milestone chart, detailed stage breakdowns, and actionable next steps.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block above.
2. Fill in your current title, long-term target role, skills, and industry.
3. Run the prompt in your AI model.
4. Review the stage breakdown and integrate immediate skill targets into your current learning plan.

## Expected Output

A multi-stage career trajectory document featuring a visual ASCII milestone chart, skill acquisition goals, and project recommendations.

## Tips

- Share your intermediate career goals with your current manager during annual performance reviews to align internal growth opportunities.
- Review your roadmap every 6 months to adjust for changing market conditions or personal interests.

## Notes

- Having a clear career roadmap prevents stagnation and guides focused skill acquisition.

# Freelance Project Proposal Generator

> Draft tailored freelance project proposals that address client job requirements directly, outline a solution workflow, and win contracts.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt crafts a custom project proposal in response to a client's job post. It opens by restating the client's problem, outlines your proposed approach, provides a short relevant case study, and poses a clarifying question to initiate a discussion.

## Best For

- Submitting proposals on platforms like Upwork, Freelancer, or job boards.
- Responding to inbound project inquiries with structured project scopes.
- Avoiding generic copy-paste proposals that get ignored by clients.

## Information You Need

- Client Job Posting Text / Project Brief
- Your Relevant Past Experience / Case Study
- Proposed Solution Steps / Deliverables
- Key Clarifying Question to Ask Client

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as a successful freelance consultant and sales strategist.

TASK:
Write a winning, customized project proposal responding directly to the client's job posting.

INPUT DATA:
- Client Job Post: [JOB_DESCRIPTION]
- My Proposed Solution / Workflow: [PROPOSED_SOLUTION]
- Relevant Case Study / Evidence: [CLIENT_OUTCOMES]
- Clarifying Question to Ask: [CLARIFYING_QUESTION]

PROPOSAL STRUCTURE:
1. Understanding the Goal (Lines 1-2): Rephrase the client's core problem in your own words to prove comprehension.
2. The Solution & Deliverables: Outline your step-by-step methodology to solve their issue.
3. Proof & Relevant Experience: Briefly reference 1 past project where you solved a similar problem successfully.
4. Call to Action & Question: Close with a relevant project question and invite them to chat.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Do NOT use generic greetings like "Dear Hiring Manager" or "I am an expert with 10 years of experience".
- Keep proposal concise (150–250 words max).
- Do not fabricate case study metrics.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Full copy-paste project proposal formatted in clean Markdown.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block.
2. Insert the client job post, your solution steps, case study, and project question.
3. Run the prompt in your AI model.
4. Copy the customized proposal into your bid submission window.

## Expected Output

A concise proposal addressing client goals, step-by-step workflow, proof of competence, and engagement question.

## Tips

- The first 2 lines of your proposal determine whether a client opens your bid—make sure they focus entirely on the client's project.
- Asking a thoughtful project-specific question encourages clients to reply to start a message thread.

## Notes

- Customized proposals addressing specific job details win contracts far more consistently than long generic templates.

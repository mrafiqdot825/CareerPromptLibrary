# Complete Interview Preparation Plan

> Generate a structured, 360-degree interview preparation plan covering company background, likely questions, story banks, and key technical concepts.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt synthesizes your background and target job description into a comprehensive interview briefing document. It identifies likely behavioral and technical questions, outlines core themes to emphasize, and suggests key talking points.

## Best For

- Preparing for upcoming recruiter screens, hiring manager interviews, or panel rounds.
- Organizing your thoughts and past project stories before interview calls.
- Gaining confidence through structured preparation.

## Information You Need

- Target Job Title & Level
- Target Company Name & Sector
- Job Description Text
- Candidate Background / Experience Notes

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as an executive interview coach and senior hiring manager.

TASK:
Develop a comprehensive interview preparation plan tailored to the target role at [COMPANY_NAME].

INPUT DATA:
- Target Job Title: [TARGET_JOB_TITLE]
- Company Name: [COMPANY_NAME]
- Job Posting Summary: [JOB_DESCRIPTION]
- My Background Overview: [WORK_EXPERIENCE]

PREPARATION PLAN SECTIONS:
1. Role Core Themes & Priorities: What the hiring team cares about most.
2. Top 5 Behavioral Questions Likely to be Asked (with suggested answer themes).
3. Top 5 Role-Specific Technical / Functional Questions (with key concepts to mention).
4. Strategic Story Bank Framework: Recommended past experiences/projects from my background to use as answers.
5. "Tell Me About Yourself" Pitch Script (60–90 second tailored response).
6. Smart Questions to Ask the Interviewer.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Keep advice practical, structured, and easy to memorize.
- Base question predictions directly on the job description requirements.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Full interview prep guide formatted cleanly in Markdown with bold headers and bullet points.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block.
2. Fill in `[TARGET_JOB_TITLE]`, `[COMPANY_NAME]`, `[JOB_DESCRIPTION]`, and `[WORK_EXPERIENCE]`.
3. Run the prompt in your AI assistant.
4. Review the briefing document and practice speaking your answers aloud.

## Expected Output

A 6-part interview briefing packet covering question predictions, story bank outlines, elevator pitch, and strategic questions.

## Tips

- Practice delivering your 90-second "Tell Me About Yourself" pitch out loud 3 times before your interview.
- Have 2 specific project stories ready that demonstrate overcoming a major obstacle.

## Notes

- Thorough preparation reduces interview nerves and improves answer structure significantly.

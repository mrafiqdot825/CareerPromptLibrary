# Standard Cover Letter Generator

> Generate a concise, professional cover letter that connects your background to a target position without repeating your resume word-for-word.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt drafts a tailored 3-paragraph cover letter based on your background and target role. It opens with a strong hook, connects key accomplishments to role requirements, and concludes with a polite call to action.

## Best For

- General job applications requiring a cover letter.
- Creating a versatile template that can be quickly adapted for multiple roles.
- Communicating professional enthusiasm without sounding overly aggressive or generic.

## Information You Need

- Your Full Name & Contact Info
- Target Job Title
- Target Company Name
- Key Relevant Experience & Top Skills
- Target Job Description

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as an expert career advisor and professional copywriter.

TASK:
Write a clean, compelling 3-paragraph cover letter tailored to the specified job role and company.

INPUT DATA:
- Candidate Name: [YOUR_NAME]
- Target Job Title: [TARGET_JOB_TITLE]
- Company Name: [COMPANY_NAME]
- Key Skills & Accomplishments: [YOUR_RELEVANT_SKILLS]
- Relevant Experience Summary: [YOUR_EXPERIENCE]
- Job Posting Summary: [JOB_DESCRIPTION]

STRUCTURE & TONE DIRECTIVES:
1. PARAGRAPH 1 (Hook & Purpose): Express clear interest in the role, state the target title, and mention 1 core reason why your background aligns.
2. PARAGRAPH 2 (Value Alignment): Connect 2 specific achievements or skills from your experience directly to key requirements in the job posting.
3. PARAGRAPH 3 (Closing & Call to Action): Reiterate enthusiasm, state willingness to discuss qualifications further in an interview, and thank the hiring team.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Do NOT use generic openings like "I am writing to express my enthusiastic interest in...".
- Do NOT repeat resume bullet points verbatim; frame details as a cohesive story of value.
- Do NOT fabricate achievements or company facts.
- Keep total word count between 200 and 300 words.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Standard business letter format in clean Markdown.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block above.
2. Replace placeholders with your information.
3. Run the prompt in your AI model.
4. Copy output into your letter template and verify tone.

## Expected Output

A complete 3-paragraph professional cover letter in standard business Markdown format.

## Tips

- Address the letter to a specific hiring manager if their name is available on LinkedIn or the job posting.
- Keep paragraphs brief to invite quick scanning by recruiters.

## Notes

- A short, well-targeted cover letter is far more effective than a lengthy, generic letter.

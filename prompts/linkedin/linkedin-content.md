# LinkedIn Content & Post Generator

> Write authentic, engaging LinkedIn posts to showcase projects, share industry learnings, or announce career milestones.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt generates 3 distinct LinkedIn post drafts based on a project milestone, industry reflection, or career update. It structures posts with strong attention hooks, short scannable lines, engaging takeaways, and relevant hashtags.

## Best For

- Building professional visibility and personal brand awareness in your industry.
- Sharing recent project completions, code repos, or case studies.
- Engaging with peers and recruiters through consistent profile activity.

## Information You Need

- Post Topic / Project Name
- Key Key Learnings or Results
- Target Audience (Peers, Recruiters, Industry Professionals)
- Call to Action (e.g., Ask a question, invite comments, link to portfolio)

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as a personal brand content strategist and LinkedIn ghostwriter.

TASK:
Write 3 engaging LinkedIn post options based on my recent professional achievement or topic context.

INPUT DATA:
- Topic / Milestone / Project: [TOPIC_OR_PROJECT]
- Core Learnings / Impact: [KEY_RESULTS]
- Target Industry: [TARGET_INDUSTRY]
- Call to Action: [CALL_TO_ACTION]

POST VARIATIONS:
1. Option 1: Storytelling & Career Reflection Hook (Focus on lessons learned).
2. Option 2: Project Breakdown & Actionable Framework (Focus on methodology and technical details).
3. Option 3: Short & Punchy Announcement (Focus on quick engagement).

POST FORMAT RULES:
- Use short sentences with line breaks after every 1-2 sentences for mobile readability.
- Start with a strong first line (no generic openings like "I am excited to announce...").
- End with an engaging question to foster comments.
- Include 3-5 relevant hashtags at the bottom.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Keep tone humble, authentic, professional, and insightful.
- Avoid clickbait tactics or artificial engagement baiting.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Markdown formatted text with 3 distinct post drafts clearly labeled.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block above.
2. Insert your topic, results, and call to action into the placeholders.
3. Generate the post options in your AI tool.
4. Select your preferred option, refine details, and post on LinkedIn.

## Expected Output

Three scannable LinkedIn post drafts optimized for mobile feed readability, complete with strong hooks and hashtags.

## Tips

- Include a visual element (image, project architecture diagram, slide deck PDF) with your post to increase feed reach.
- Reply to comments within the first 2 hours of posting to boost feed algorithm distribution.

## Notes

- Consistent posting (1-2 times per week) dramatically increases inbound recruiter profile views.

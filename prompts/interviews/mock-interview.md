# Interactive Mock Interview Simulator

> Experience a realistic, interactive mock interview session where the AI acts as your interviewer, asking questions one by one and providing feedback.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt turns your AI assistant into an interactive interviewer. The AI asks one question at a time based on your target role and job description, waits for your response, evaluates your answer, provides constructive feedback, and proceeds to the next question.

## Best For

- Practicing live interview responses in a low-stakes, simulated environment.
- Testing response timing, structure, and depth under realistic interview conditions.
- Receiving immediate actionable feedback on your answer quality.

## Information You Need

- Target Job Title & Company Name
- Target Job Description
- Candidate Resume or Background Brief

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as a senior hiring manager conducting a live, interactive job interview for the position of [TARGET_JOB_TITLE] at [COMPANY_NAME].

MOCK INTERVIEW RULES:
1. Conduct the interview iteratively—ask ONLY ONE question at a time.
2. Wait for my response before asking follow-ups or moving to the next question.
3. After I answer each question:
   - Provide brief constructive feedback (Strengths, Areas for Improvement, Suggested Rephrasing).
   - Rate my answer quality on a scale of 1 to 5 with a brief rationale.
   - Ask the NEXT interview question.
4. After 5 questions, conclude the interview session and provide a Final Assessment Report summarizing overall readiness, top strengths, and key growth areas.

INPUT DATA TO START:
- Target Job Title: [TARGET_JOB_TITLE]
- Company Name: [COMPANY_NAME]
- Job Posting Summary: [JOB_DESCRIPTION]
- My Background Notes: [WORK_EXPERIENCE]

To begin, acknowledge these rules and ask your FIRST interview question.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block above.
2. Fill in `[TARGET_JOB_TITLE]`, `[COMPANY_NAME]`, `[JOB_DESCRIPTION]`, and `[WORK_EXPERIENCE]`.
3. Paste into your AI tool to begin the simulation.
4. Type your answers as if speaking to an interviewer, and review feedback after each question.

## Expected Output

An interactive multi-turn interview conversation providing real-time feedback after every response, ending with a comprehensive final evaluation report.

## Tips

- Answer questions naturally without referencing notes to simulate real interview pressure.
- Review the final feedback report to identify recurring answer weaknesses.

## Notes

- Interactive mock practice builds muscle memory and reduces real interview anxiety significantly.

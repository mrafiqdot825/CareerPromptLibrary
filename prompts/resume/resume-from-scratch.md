# Resume From Scratch Generator

> Build a structured, professional resume from unorganized work history, notes, and raw career details.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt takes unstructured bullet points, past job duties, and general notes, and transforms them into a clean, modern, multi-section resume. It structures raw information into professional summary, work experience, education, and skills sections.

## Best For

- Creating your very first professional resume.
- Updating a long-neglected resume with scattered notes.
- Recent graduates or individuals organizing scattered job experiences.

## Information You Need

- Full Name & Contact Info
- Desired Job Title / Field
- List of Past Employers, Dates, and Roles
- Key Duties & Projects per Role
- Schooling / Degrees
- Technical, Software, or Language Skills

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as a professional resume writer and career development coach.

TASK:
Convert my raw, unformatted background information into a complete, professional, well-structured resume.

INPUT DATA:
- Full Name: [YOUR_NAME]
- Contact Information: [CONTACT_INFORMATION]
- Target Job Title / Industry: [TARGET_JOB_TITLE]
- Raw Experience & Past Duties: [WORK_EXPERIENCE]
- Education & Degrees: [EDUCATION]
- Known Skills & Tools: [SKILLS]
- Certifications / Volunteering / Projects: [PROJECTS]

CONSTRAINTS:
1. Do not invent any companies, degrees, dates, or fake experience.
2. Structure the content logically with clear headings: Summary, Core Competencies, Professional Experience, Education.
3. Transform vague task descriptions into concise, professional bullet points starting with strong action verbs (e.g., Developed, Managed, Coordinated, Optimized).
4. Keep tone professional, confident, and direct.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Provide the full resume text formatted cleanly in Markdown, followed by 3-5 quick suggestions on where to expand details.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block above.
2. Replace the bracketed placeholders with your raw background notes.
3. Paste into your AI assistant.
4. Copy the generated Markdown into your word processor and adjust formatting.

## Expected Output

A complete, clean, professional resume formatted in standard Markdown sections with strong action verbs and clean layout structure.

## Tips

- Include informal or freelance experience if transitioning into a new role.
- Be as detailed as possible in the raw notes so the AI has rich content to draw from.

## Notes

- Review generated bullet points to make sure they accurately capture your actual level of responsibility.

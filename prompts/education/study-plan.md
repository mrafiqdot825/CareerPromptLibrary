# Structured Technical Study Plan Generator

> Build a customized, week-by-week study schedule tailored to your exam date, daily availability, and learning preferences.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt breaks down complex exam or certification syllabi (e.g., AWS Certified Solutions Architect, PMP, Data Structures & Algorithms, CPA) into a balanced, week-by-week study schedule complete with review sessions and practice quizzes.

## Best For

- Preparing for industry certifications or academic exams.
- Managing study time alongside full-time work or university schedules.
- Avoiding cramming through structured spaced-repetition learning.

## Information You Need

- Certification / Exam Name
- Syllabus or Key Subject Topics
- Total Weeks Until Exam
- Weekly Study Hours Available

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as an expert academic tutor and learning specialist.

TASK:
Develop a structured, week-by-week study plan for [EXAM_OR_SUBJECT_NAME] tailored to my target test date and weekly available hours.

INPUT DATA:
- Subject / Exam Name: [EXAM_OR_SUBJECT_NAME]
- Target Exam Date / Timeline: [TARGET_TIMELINE]
- Weekly Hours Available: [WEEKLY_HOURS_AVAILABLE]
- Primary Topics / Syllabus: [SYLLABUS_TOPICS]
- Current Mastery Level: [CURRENT_KNOWLEDGE_LEVEL]

STUDY PLAN STRUCTURE:
1. Phase 1: Core Concept Mastery (Breakdown of syllabus topics across weeks 1-X).
2. Phase 2: Practice & Application (Exercises, lab work, practice tests).
3. Phase 3: Final Review & Exam Simulation (Mock exams and targeted weak-area remediation).
4. Weekly Breakdown Table: Specific topics, learning goals, and practice tasks per week.
5. Spaced Repetition & Revision Protocol.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Keep weekly study workloads realistic and balanced.
- Include dedicated rest and review days to prevent burnout.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Markdown study guide with a weekly breakdown schedule and daily study session routine recommendations.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block.
2. Fill in the exam name, timeline, available hours, syllabus topics, and mastery level.
3. Generate the study plan in your AI assistant.
4. Block off scheduled study hours in your calendar.

## Expected Output

A phased week-by-week study schedule featuring topic assignments, practice milestones, and review protocols.

## Tips

- Incorporate active recall (flashcards, practice questions) rather than passive reading.
- Adjust study duration based on topic difficulty.

## Notes

- Consistent daily study (e.g., 1 hour/day) produces far higher retention than weekend cram sessions.

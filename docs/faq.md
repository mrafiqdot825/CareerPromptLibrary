# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is CareerPromptLibrary?
**CareerPromptLibrary** is a curated, open-source collection of structured AI prompts designed to assist job seekers, professionals, students, and freelancers with resume tailoring, cover letter drafting, interview prep, career planning, and workplace communication.

---

### How do I use a prompt?
1. Find the prompt matching your current task in `prompts/`.
2. Copy the text block inside the **Copy & Paste Prompt** section.
3. Replace all bracketed placeholders (such as `[YOUR_NAME]` or `[JOB_DESCRIPTION]`) with your actual data.
4. Paste the completed prompt into your AI model of choice (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, etc.).

---

### Which AI tools are supported?
All prompts are **model-agnostic**. They work effectively with:
- ChatGPT (GPT-4o, GPT-4o-mini, GPT-3.5)
- Claude (Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Claude 3 Opus, Claude 3 Haiku)
- Google Gemini (Gemini 1.5 Pro, Gemini 1.5 Flash)
- DeepSeek, Meta Llama 3, Mistral, and other open LLMs.

---

### Can AI guarantee me a job or interview?
**No.** AI tools can help format, structure, and polish your application materials, but hiring decisions depend on real-world qualification, market conditions, networking, and interview performance. Prompts in this library are designed to present your true experience clearly and professionally, without fake metrics or false guarantees.

---

### Can I modify these prompts?
**Yes.** All prompts are released under the open-source MIT License. You are free to adapt, customize, and adjust prompts for your personal needs or professional workflows.

---

### Are these prompts free to use commercially?
**Yes.** Under the MIT License, you can use these prompts freely for personal, corporate, or commercial projects.

---

### Should I verify AI-generated text before submitting applications?
**Always.** AI models can make mistakes or misinterpret context. Review every generated document for accuracy, correct dates, truthful achievement metrics, and authentic tone before sending it to potential employers or clients.

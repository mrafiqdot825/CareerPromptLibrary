# Getting Started with CareerPromptLibrary

Welcome to **CareerPromptLibrary**! This guide walks you through using our prompt collection effectively to accelerate your job search, build ATS-friendly application materials, prepare for interviews, and advance your professional growth.

---

## Simple Step-by-Step Workflow

```text
Choose a Prompt
       │
       ▼
Gather Information & Copy Template
       │
       ▼
Replace Placeholders ([YOUR_NAME], etc.)
       │
       ▼
Paste into AI Assistant
       │
       ▼
Review & Verify Truthfulness
       │
       ▼
Use Final Output
```

---

## Step 1: Choose the Right Prompt

Navigate the prompt directories based on your immediate need:

- **Resume Building & Tailoring**: Look in [`prompts/resume/`](../prompts/resume/)
- **Cover Letters**: Look in [`prompts/cover-letter/`](../prompts/cover-letter/)
- **Job Posting Analysis & Fit Assessment**: Look in [`prompts/job-search/`](../prompts/job-search/)
- **LinkedIn Profiles & Content**: Look in [`prompts/linkedin/`](../prompts/linkedin/)
- **Interview Preparation & Mock Practice**: Look in [`prompts/interviews/`](../prompts/interviews/)

- **Academic & Skill Development**: Look in [`prompts/education/`](../prompts/education/)

- **Workplace Communication & Emails**: Look in [`prompts/workplace/`](../prompts/workplace/)

---

## Step 2: Gather Your Information

Before opening your AI tool:
1. Open [`templates/resume-input-template.md`](../templates/resume-input-template.md) to gather your skills, achievements, and work history.
2. Open [`templates/job-description-template.md`](../templates/job-description-template.md) to clean up target job postings.

---

## Step 3: Fill Out Placeholders

Every prompt uses standard uppercase placeholders inside square brackets:
- `[YOUR_NAME]`
- `[TARGET_JOB_TITLE]`
- `[COMPANY_NAME]`
- `[WORK_EXPERIENCE]`
- `[JOB_DESCRIPTION]`

Copy the prompt block into a text editor or directly into your AI window, and replace each bracketed placeholder with your actual details.

---

## Step 4: Run the Prompt in Your AI Assistant

Paste the customized prompt into your AI model of choice:
- **ChatGPT** (GPT-4o, GPT-4o-mini)
- **Claude** (Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Claude 3 Opus)
- **Gemini** (Gemini 1.5 Pro, Gemini 1.5 Flash)
- **DeepSeek / Llama 3 / Mistral**

All prompts in this library are **model-agnostic** and do not rely on proprietary AI features.

---

## Step 5: Review & Fact-Check

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Never copy AI output blindly.**
> AI models can hallucinate metrics or smooth over details. Verify that:
> 1. Dates, titles, and company names match your real background.
> 2. Numbers and achievement metrics are accurate and truthful.
> 3. The tone sounds authentic and natural.

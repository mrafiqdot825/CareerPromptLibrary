# Resume Tailoring Assistant

> Perform a targeted overhaul of your resume for a specific job posting, reprioritizing experience and bullet points to match the role's core requirements.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt conducts a deep structural alignment of your resume against a target job posting. It re-orders bullet points to emphasize relevant experience, aligns technical terminology, and ensures your top achievements directly match the employer's key pain points.

## Best For

- Customizing a general master resume for a dream job application.
- Pivoting between slightly different role titles (e.g., Project Manager vs Scrum Master).
- Ensuring your resume highlights your most relevant experience on page one.

## Information You Need

- Master Resume Content
- Target Job Title & Job Description
- Target Company Name & Industry

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as a senior executive hiring consultant and master resume strategist.

TASK:
Tailor my master resume specifically for the target position at [COMPANY_NAME], reprioritizing accomplishments and aligning terminology with the job description.

INPUT DATA:
- Target Company: [COMPANY_NAME]
- Target Job Title: [TARGET_JOB_TITLE]
- Job Posting Text: [JOB_DESCRIPTION]
- Master Resume Content: [WORK_EXPERIENCE]

TAILORING STEPS:
1. Re-order and prioritize experience bullet points so that the most relevant duties appear first.
2. Emphasize transferable skills and relevant achievements that match the primary responsibilities listed in the job description.
3. Adjust summary and core competencies section to reflect the company's language and key requirements.
4. Prune or condense irrelevant details to keep the focus sharp and impact high.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Do not fabricate experiences, credentials, or metrics.
- Keep output clear, professional, and formatted in standard Markdown.
- Provide a brief summary of structural adjustments made.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
1. Tailoring Strategy Overview (Key adjustments made and why)
2. Complete Tailored Resume Document
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block above.
2. Fill in `[COMPANY_NAME]`, `[TARGET_JOB_TITLE]`, `[JOB_DESCRIPTION]`, and `[WORK_EXPERIENCE]`.
3. Run the prompt in your AI tool.
4. Review the tailored output and save as a custom resume version for that application.

## Expected Output

A strategy overview explaining the restructuring logic, followed by a complete, tailored resume ready for application submission.

## Tips

- Maintain a "master resume" containing all your historical roles, then use this prompt to generate role-specific versions.
- Save each tailored version with the company name in the file title (e.g., `Resume_JohnDoe_AcmeCorp.pdf`).

## Notes

- Tailoring increases response rates significantly compared to submitting generic resumes.

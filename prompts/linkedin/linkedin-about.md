# LinkedIn About Section Generator

> Write an engaging first-person career story for your LinkedIn About section that hooks readers, showcases expertise, and invites connection.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt crafts a 250–350 word first-person About summary. It starts with an attention-grabbing hook, highlights your career journey and core strengths, formats key technical skills in a bulleted list for easy scanning, and ends with a clear invitation to connect.

## Best For

- Creating an authentic, human personal narrative for your LinkedIn profile.
- Explaining your career trajectory, career shifts, or passion for your field.
- Making your profile memorable to visiting hiring managers and partners.

## Information You Need

- Current / Target Role
- Years of Experience & Major Milestones
- Core Competencies & Primary Tools
- Preferred Professional Tone (e.g., Warm & Personable, Executive & Strategic, Technical & Direct)

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as a personal brand storyteller and career copywriter.

TASK:
Write a compelling, first-person narrative for my LinkedIn About section that connects my career journey with my core skills and target goals.

INPUT DATA:
- Target Job Title: [TARGET_JOB_TITLE]
- Industry / Sector: [TARGET_INDUSTRY]
- Years of Experience: [YEARS_OF_EXPERIENCE]
- Primary Skills & Expertise: [SKILLS]
- Key Career Highlights / Projects: [ACHIEVEMENTS]
- Preferred Tone: [PREFERRED_TONE]

SECTION STRUCTURE:
1. The Hook (Lines 1-2): A strong opening statement that makes the reader click "...see more".
2. The Narrative (Paragraph 1-2): A short story describing your passion, domain expertise, and core problem-solving focus.
3. Core Specialties (Bulleted List): Clean bullet points listing 6-8 core technical or domain competencies.
4. Call to Action (Closing Line): A friendly line welcoming networking outreach, connection requests, or career opportunities.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Use first-person perspective ("I", "my").
- Do NOT fabricate achievements, experience, or degrees.
- Avoid robotic corporate clichés.
- Length: 250 to 350 words total.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Full LinkedIn About text ready to copy-paste.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block above.
2. Fill in your background info and preferred tone.
3. Generate the response in your AI assistant.
4. Review for voice authenticity and paste into your profile.

## Expected Output

A complete, formatted first-person LinkedIn About section with opening hook, career story, formatted skill list, and call to action.

## Tips

- Keep the first 2 lines very punchy because LinkedIn truncates text behind a "...see more" button.
- Include an email address in the final line if you want recruiters to contact you directly without InMail.

## Notes

- An engaging About section transforms your profile from a static resume copy into an active professional bio.

# Application Strategy Optimizer

> Plan the optimal execution strategy for a target job opportunity, including employee referral outreach, application timing, and follow-up templates.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt helps you maximize the impact of every job application. It constructs a 3-stage application strategy: warm referral identification, recruiter message drafting, and a systematic post-application follow-up schedule.

## Best For

- Standing out when submitting applications to highly competitive companies.
- Leveraging professional networks to secure internal referrals.
- Following up professionally without annoying hiring teams.

## Information You Need

- Target Job Title & Company Name
- Target Job Posting Link or Overview
- Current Connection Status (e.g., Cold outreach, 2nd-degree connection, Alum)

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as a talent acquisition director and professional networking strategist.

TASK:
Build a complete application execution strategy for a target position, including networking scripts, timing strategy, and follow-up cadences.

INPUT DATA:
- Target Job Title: [TARGET_JOB_TITLE]
- Company Name: [COMPANY_NAME]
- Job Posting Summary: [JOB_DESCRIPTION]
- Connection Type: [CONNECTION_STATUS]

STRATEGY STEPS:
1. Referral Outreach Script: Short outreach message to an employee or alum at [COMPANY_NAME] requesting a brief chat or referral.
2. Direct Recruiter Message: Brief note to the hiring manager or recruiter accompanying the application.
3. Follow-Up Schedule & Templates:
   - Follow-up 1 (5–7 days post-application)
   - Follow-up 2 (10–14 days post-application)
4. Application Best Practices & Submission Timing Advice.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Messages must be short, courteous, professional, and easy to read.
- Do not sound pushy or overly demanding.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Markdown execution guide with copy-paste message templates clearly labeled.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block above.
2. Fill in `[TARGET_JOB_TITLE]`, `[COMPANY_NAME]`, `[JOB_DESCRIPTION]`, and `[CONNECTION_STATUS]`.
3. Run the prompt in your AI model.
4. Use the generated scripts for LinkedIn outreach and email follow-ups.

## Expected Output

Custom outreach scripts and a step-by-step follow-up timeline designed for your target company.

## Tips

- Search LinkedIn for company employees who attended your university or share previous employers.
- Always customize cold messages with a genuine detail about the recipient's background.

## Notes

- Internal employee referrals carry significantly higher interview conversion rates than unreferred portal submissions.

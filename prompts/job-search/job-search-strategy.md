# Job Search Strategy Planner

> Develop a structured, weekly job search action plan covering target company identification, networking, job boards, and application tracking.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt generates a strategic roadmap for your job search campaign. It defines primary application channels, identifies target company tiers, sets weekly activity targets, and suggests networking outreach tactics.

## Best For

- Anyone starting a new job search or feeling stuck in a slow search.
- Organizing weekly effort across direct applications, referrals, and networking.
- Avoiding application burnout through structured routine management.

## Information You Need

- Target Job Title & Level
- Target Location / Remote Preference
- Industry / Sector Focus
- Time Available Per Week for Search

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as a senior career coach and job search strategist.

TASK:
Develop a structured, multi-channel job search strategy tailored to my target role, experience level, and weekly availability.

INPUT DATA:
- Target Job Title: [TARGET_JOB_TITLE]
- Industry / Sector: [TARGET_INDUSTRY]
- Experience Level: [YEARS_OF_EXPERIENCE]
- Location Preference: [LOCATION_PREFERENCE]
- Hours Available Per Week: [WEEKLY_HOURS_AVAILABLE]

STRATEGY FRAMEWORK:
1. Target Company Tiering (Tier 1: Dream Companies, Tier 2: Solid Fits, Tier 3: Safety/Volume Fits).
2. Channel Distribution (Percentage of time spent on direct networking, company career portals, job boards, and recruiter outreach).
3. Weekly Activity Blueprint (Specific weekly targets for outreach messages, tailored applications, and follow-ups).
4. Networking Tactics (Whom to reach out to and key conversation starters).
5. Application Tracker Setup Recommendations.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Keep recommendations practical, actionable, and structured by week.
- Do not promise unrealistic outcomes (e.g., "guaranteed job in 14 days").

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Provide the full job search plan in Markdown with clear tables and weekly activity checklists.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block.
2. Replace placeholders with your role goals and weekly schedule.
3. Run the prompt in your AI assistant.
4. Implement the weekly activity blueprint in your calendar.

## Expected Output

A comprehensive job search strategy document featuring target company categories, effort allocation percentages, and a weekly action plan.

## Tips

- Allocate at least 40% of your search time to warm networking and outreach rather than relying solely on cold online applications.
- Set up a spreadsheet or Trello board to log contacts and follow-up dates.

## Notes

- A structured search strategy dramatically reduces job search anxiety and keeps momentum consistent.

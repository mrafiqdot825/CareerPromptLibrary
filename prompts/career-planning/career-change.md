# Career Pivot & Transition Planner

> Map out a career transition strategy that identifies transferable skills, addresses skill gaps, and frames past experience for a new industry.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt helps you transition smoothly into a new field or role. It audits your current experience to extract transferable skills, identifies critical new knowledge to acquire, and provides positioning strategies to explain your career pivot convincingly to hiring managers.

## Best For

- Transitioning into a new industry (e.g., Finance to Tech, Teaching to Instructional Design, Sales to Product Management).
- Reframing non-traditional experience for target hiring teams.
- Minimizing salary drops during career shifts by emphasizing transferable value.

## Information You Need

- Current Role & Industry Background
- Desired Target Role & Target Industry
- Core Transferable Skills (e.g., project management, data analysis, client communication)
- Time Available for Upskilling / Re-education

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as a career transition specialist and executive pivot coach.

TASK:
Develop a strategic career transition plan to pivot from my current field into my target role, highlighting transferable value and bridging skill gaps.

INPUT DATA:
- Current Role & Field: [WORK_EXPERIENCE]
- Target Role & Industry: [TARGET_JOB_TITLE]
- Core transferable Skills: [SKILLS]
- Educational Background: [EDUCATION]
- Time Frame for Transition: [WEEKLY_HOURS_AVAILABLE]

PIVOT PLAN STRUCTURE:
1. Transferable Skills Inventory: Mapping current skills to target role requirements.
2. Skill & Knowledge Gap Analysis: Critical hard skills/tools to acquire before applying.
3. The Positioning Narrative: A compelling 3-sentence personal story explaining "Why I am pivoting and why my background is a unique asset".
4. Stepping-Stone / Bridging Projects: Portfolio projects or volunteer work to prove competence in the new domain.
5. Resume & LinkedIn Re-positioning Strategy.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Provide practical, risk-managed advice.
- Avoid unrealistic claims about instant transition without necessary upskilling.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Markdown transition blueprint with structured action steps, narrative scripts, and skill gap tables.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block.
2. Fill in your current background, target role, skills, and time frame.
3. Run the prompt in your AI model.
4. Implement the positioning narrative across your resume summary and LinkedIn About section.

## Expected Output

A comprehensive pivot blueprint including a transferable skill matrix, gap breakdown, portfolio project ideas, and positioning narrative.

## Tips

- Build 1-2 tangible portfolio projects in your target field before sending out applications to prove hands-on capability.
- Network with people who successfully made the exact same pivot to gather real-world advice.

## Notes

- Career transitions are built on transferable skills and evidence of self-directed learning.

# Career Opportunity Analyzer

> Compare job offers or potential career opportunities against compensation, career trajectory, work-life balance, and long-term goals.

## What This Prompt Does

This prompt performs an objective comparative analysis between job offers or potential opportunities. It evaluates key criteria including total compensation, career advancement potential, learning curve, team stability, and work-life flexibility.

## Best For

- Choosing between multiple job offers.
- Evaluating whether to leave a current role for a new opportunity.
- Preparing questions to clarify key unknowns before accepting an offer.

## Information You Need

- Role Details for Opportunity A (and Opportunity B, if comparing)
- Base Salary, Bonus, Benefits, & Perks Data
- Current Career Goals & Personal Priorities

## Copy & Paste Prompt

```text
Act as a senior executive career strategist and compensation analyst.

TASK:
Analyze and compare the provided career opportunities against my professional goals, compensation requirements, and personal priorities.

INPUT DATA:
- My Core Priorities & Career Goals: [CAREER_GOALS]
- Opportunity A Details: [OPPORTUNITY_A_DETAILS]
- Opportunity B / Current Role Details: [OPPORTUNITY_B_DETAILS]

EVALUATION CRITERIA:
1. Total Compensation & Benefits Package Breakdown.
2. Career Advancement & Skill Growth Potential.
3. Work-Life Balance, Flexibility, & Remote Culture.
4. Risk Profile & Company Stability Analysis.
5. Alignment Matrix with Long-Term Goals.
6. Recommended Clarifying Questions to Ask Employers Before Accepting.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Provide an objective, balanced evaluation highlighting trade-offs for each option.
- Do not make absolute decisions; present structured analytical clarity so I can make an informed choice.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Comparison matrix table followed by analytical trade-off breakdowns and next-step recommendations in Markdown.
```

## How to Use

1. Copy the prompt block.
2. Enter your career goals and opportunity details into the placeholders.
3. Generate the evaluation report in your AI tool.
4. Use the generated clarifying questions during offer negotiation calls.

## Expected Output

A comparative analysis matrix highlighting pros, cons, trade-offs, and critical questions to ask before deciding.

## Tips

- Look beyond base salary to consider equity, health benefits, PTO, learning stipends, and commute time.
- Consider which opportunity provides the strongest story for your next career move 3 years from now.

## Notes

- An objective evaluation matrix removes emotional overload during major career decisions.
